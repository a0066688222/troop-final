Troop 350

Troop 350 is a scout led (means the scouts run most of it.)

About Our Troop

Troop 350 follows the Scout Law and Scout Oath with honor.
We have meetings every Wednesday, however, these meetings can be canceled at short notice.

Why You Should Join us

Our troop offers camping trips frequently, many activities, and you will be treated as a fellow scout instead of a stranger.
All of our scouts are friendly and none of us bite.

Ranks

The ranks of Boy Scouts is:

  1. Scout
  2. Tenderfoot
  3. Second class
  4. First class
  5. Star
  6. Life
  7. Eagle
